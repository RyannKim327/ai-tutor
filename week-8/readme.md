# Understanding Positive and Negative Numbers

In our daily lives, we often encounter situations that can be represented by numbers. Some numbers are positive, like when you have money, and others are negative, like when you owe money. Understanding positive and negative numbers is like understanding whether a situation is a gain or a loss. 

### What Are Positive and Negative Numbers?
- **Positive Numbers:** These are numbers greater than zero. Think of these as having something, like having three apples. In number terms, we write this as +3 or simply 3.
- **Negative Numbers:** These are numbers less than zero. You can think of them as debts or losses, like owing three apples. In number terms, we write this as -3.
- **Zero:** This number is neutral; it’s neither positive nor negative. It represents a balance, like having no apples at all.

### Visualization with a Number Line
Imagine a straight line that stretches infinitely in both directions. In the center of this line is zero. To the right of zero are positive numbers, and to the left are negative numbers. This visualization helps you understand that:
- The further you go to the right, the larger the positive number.
- The further you go to the left, the larger the negative number (or, in other words, the more you owe).

### How to Think About This
When you think about numbers, start by identifying whether they are positive, negative, or zero. This helps you categorize different situations in everyday life. For instance, if you have $5 and owe $3, you can think of it as $5 (positive) - $3 (negative) = $2 (positive).
