# Understanding Simple Patterns

In our day-to-day lives, we often encounter patterns. A pattern is simply a sequence that follows a specific rule. For example, when you see a pattern like 1, 2, 3, you notice it increases by 1 each time. Think about how you might create a necklace with beads: if you decide to use red, blue, red, blue, this is a repeating pattern.

### The Learning Concept
When we work with numbers, patterns can be identified through sequences like 1, 3, 5 or geometric sequences like 2, 4, 8. Recognizing these patterns helps in predicting what comes next, much like finishing a puzzle where you can guess the missing pieces based on what you can see.

### How to Think
1. **Observe**: Start by looking at the numbers or objects and see how they relate. Are they increasing or decreasing? Are they alternating?
2. **Identify the Rule**: Find what connects them—are they all odd numbers? Are they increasing by 3 each time?
3. **Extend the Pattern**: Try to find numbers or objects that would follow the given rule to continue the series you see.
4. **Practice**: Repeat this with different sets of numbers or items to become comfortable.
