# Understanding Simple Addition

In this week, you're going to learn about **addition**, a fundamental arithmetic operation. Imagine you're at a fruit market. If you buy 3 apples and later you purchase 2 more, how many apples do you have now? To find out, you simply **combine** the two quantities by adding them together.  

So, **3 apples + 2 apples = 5 apples.**

**Key Concepts:**
- **Addition** is all about finding out how many items you have in total when you combine groups.  
- We will think about **numbers as groups of items.** 
- The symbol for addition is `+` which means you are putting things together.

In your `challenge.py`, you will:
- Start by asking the user for two numbers.
- Add those numbers together to get a total.
- Finally, show the result.

This exercise will take about **1 week** to complete as you practice and get comfortable with addition and the idea of combining numbers into a total.

Take your time to understand how addition works, and try to visualize it in real life. You can think of different scenarios, like combining different types of toys or objects. Enjoy the process!
